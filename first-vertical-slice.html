The acceptance run

Start a new profile. Move/jump through the course. Take damage and keep that reduced health through a normal area transition. Return, defeat the enemy, earn XP, and cross a level threshold. Collect the pickup and open the shortcut. Activate the checkpoint. Earn another reward after that activation, then die: the later reward must remain even though recovery occurs at the older checkpoint location.

Travel A→B→A. The ordinary enemy may reset; the pickup must stay collected and the shortcut open. Save and fully exit. Relaunch the executable and load. Verify inventory, XP/points, discovery, checkpoint, and shortcut records. Confirm that no reward or level-up point grant replays during reconstruction.

Finally, close/remove the HUD while running the slice. Gameplay and persistence must still work. This catches accidental widget authority early.

After the first slice passes, finish the ranged/projectile portion of Phase 5 before marking the phase complete. Phase 11 repeats and extends this sequence in a packaged executable, with deliberate bad data and save-generation failures.

Resource integration acceptance

The Phase 2 slice now includes BPC_Resources and three HUD vitals alongside the original movement/combat loop. Follow the exact phase configuration and resource lifecycle/API contract. If values are zero/unavailable, inspect the assigned DA_PlayerBaseStats, seven-field Stats rebuild Success, owned Resources attachment, readiness/resume order, and current HUD references before adding defaults. A capacity increase is intentionally not a refill.

Resource-free movement/basic attacks remain required. Phase 3 normal travel preserves absolute pools, death/disk load create full final pools, Phase 8 passives modify capacities/rates, and Phase 9 abilities spend only at accepted resource/charge commit. Record actual expected versus observed results, restore development overrides, and never infer Unreal execution from static documentation checks.
