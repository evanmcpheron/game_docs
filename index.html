Your first Blueprint RPG

An offline, asset-by-asset build manual for Unreal Engine 5.8.3 and Paper2D.

On this page · 5 sections

You are building a 2D side-scrolling exploration-focused action-platforming RPG: responsive platforming and real-time combat in interconnected area maps, with rooms, loops, shortcuts, backtracking and meaningful discoveries. One persistent character keeps XP, inventory, equipment, skills and lasting world changes through ordinary travel and death. It is not one seamless open world.
