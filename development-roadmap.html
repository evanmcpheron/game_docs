Build order

Development roadmap

Twelve phases, in the original order. Finish the current proof before expanding the next system.

Start with a controllable body and good movement. Prove combat, in-memory persistence and disk loading before broad RPG content. The first vertical slice comes after the melee/reward/leveling subset of Phase 5; the full ranged proof still belongs to that phase.

PHASE 00 · 8 ASSETS

Project skeleton and controlled spawning

Prove one configured player receives horizontal input in a stable side-on lab.

PHASE 01 · 16 ASSETS

Movement quality and PaperZD presentation

Prove responsive single-jump movement with buffering/coyote/cut edge cases and independent visuals.

PHASE 02 · 24 ASSETS

Health, minimal stats, actions, and combat

Prove an authoritative damage/action loop without AI or RPG complexity.

PHASE 03 · 32 ASSETS

In-memory persistence, interaction, and connected area maps

Prove lasting values survive Actor replacement during travel and death within one session.

PHASE 04 · 9 ASSETS

Disk saves, atomic pickups, and recovery

Prove a fresh process restores coherent player/world progress without duplicate claims.

PHASE 05 · 20 ASSETS

Enemies, rewards, and basic leveling

Prove readable melee/ranged threats, once-only rewards and multi-level progression integrate with persistence.

PHASE 06 · 3 ASSETS

Inventory operations and consumable use

Prove owned-entry and consumable operations work independently of the inventory screen.

PHASE 07 · 8 ASSETS

Equipment and synchronized visual layers

Prove equipment changes stats and appearance without copying ownership or compounding bonuses.

PHASE 08 · 8 ASSETS

Skill-tree rules and passive builds

Prove the skill graph validates real costs/prerequisites/exclusions before its UI asks for purchases.

PHASE 09 · 17 ASSETS

Active abilities, temporary effects, and capability gates

Prove derived ability unlocks, accepted execution, safe cancellation and timed-effect travel policy.

PHASE 10 · 16 ASSETS

NPC dialogue, menus, and presentation integration

Prove condition-sensitive NPC conversations and a complete front-end/menu lifecycle.

PHASE 11 · 2 ASSETS

Validation, recovery hardening, and packaged-build proof

Prove content, save recovery and lifecycle behavior in a fresh packaged executable before scaling.

Canonical roadmap reference · PART AI — Final project roadmap

Current guidance retains gameplay policy and incorporates the PaperZD architecture decision. Historical source text remains in the unchanged original roadmap. E-number references belong to the original roadmap; see verification notes.

Required now: the phase 0–1 foundation and movement course. Useful soon: the first persistence/combat slice through the melee portion of phase 5. Required later by the committed game: inventory/equipment, skill tree, abilities/effects, NPC dialogue, and complete presentation. Future-compatible only: extra visual movement states, additional equipment slots, additional enemy/ability definitions, and larger areas. Unnecessary until explicitly committed: optional traversal mechanics, crafting, vendors/economy, randomized loot/rarity, durability, quests/factions/reputation, respec, loadouts, fast travel, multiplayer, and speculative new gameplay systems.

The invariant throughout development is simple: definitions describe possibilities; Profile and WorldState own lasting progress; the current body executes temporary gameplay; GameInstance coordinates durable changes and recovery; UI and animation observe rather than own the rules.

Incremental integration and test-world workflow

CREATE → COMPILE → CONFIGURE → INTEGRATE → EDIT TEST WORLD → RUN → VERIFY → CONTINUE. A phase is complete only after its new runtime feature reaches the real consumer and passes a playable or controlled system scenario. Use development test environments for map roles, stable fixture identity and bootstrap boundaries.
