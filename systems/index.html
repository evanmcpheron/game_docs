Browse by system

Nineteen linked chapters explain how the architecture becomes a working game.

01

Input intent and routing

A button press is a request, not proof that the requested action is legal.

02

Movement

Make the empty obstacle course feel reliable before adding RPG breadth.

03

Animation and layered presentation

Show gameplay facts without allowing presentation to become gameplay authority.

04

Stats and build reconstruction

Calculate the current body from complete inputs without accumulating old bonuses.

05

Combat, damage and action ownership

Prove attacks against a dummy before giving an enemy a brain.

06

Persistent models and stable world identity

Keep facts when Actors disappear; reconstruct Actors without replaying rewards.

07

Interaction

Select a target, revalidate on the press, and let that target request its own operation.

08

Areas, rooms, travel and bootstrap

Use connected ordinary maps with explicit readiness and named destinations.

09

Disk saving, loading and death recovery

Snapshot coherent progress; restore a valid recovery state rather than an exact gameplay instant.

10

Enemy bodies, decisions and rewards

Share live-body components while keeping AI decisions and reward ownership separate.

11

XP and leveling

Award every crossed threshold once, and never replay awards during load.

12

Inventory and consumables

Own entries in Profile; make acquisition and use coherent operations.

13

Equipment and visible layers

Reference owned entries, rebuild their contributions and synchronize their visuals.

14

Skill graph and specialization

Implement costs, prerequisites and exclusions in the model before drawing the tree.

15

Active abilities and capability gates

Derive unlocks, accept one execution, own its recharge and cancel it safely.

16

Temporary effects

Refresh and expire runtime sources without editing permanent stats.

17

NPCs and conditional dialogue

Keep authored lines, current conversation and remembered facts in different places.

18

UI, input modes and observation

Widgets request and observe; destroying one cannot destroy gameplay state.

19

Validation and packaged proof

Prove the entire loop in a fresh process before scaling production content.

The system order is a reference route. The authoritative build order remains Phase 0 through Phase 11.
